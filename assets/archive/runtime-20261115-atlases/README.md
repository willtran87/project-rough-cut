# Full Under the Green surface atlases

These three 1254x1254 atlases were moved out of the GitHub Pages bundle on November 15, 2026. The game sampled only one quadrant of `surfaces-v1.png` (bark) and `surfaces-industrial-v1.png` (sand), and the bottom half of `surfaces-masonry-v3.png` (concrete, tile). The runtime now ships those exact tiles as `surfaces-bark-v1.png`, `surfaces-sand-v1.png` and `surfaces-concrete-tile-v3.png` in `web/assets/under-green/`; each was checked pixel-for-pixel against the crop of its source atlas.

The originals here are byte-for-byte copies and keep their embedded provenance metadata. This archive is not deployed.
