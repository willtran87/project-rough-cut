# Remaining theme and aesthetic review — 2026-11-14

Review only. No runtime changes. The current menu and shared chapter briefing/loading presentation are the visual reference: dark forest-green material, warm ivory text, restrained brass emphasis, deliberate serif headings, clear supporting text, continuous borders with chipped corners, and restrained motion. Preserve the approved intro reveal and lead-in.

## Scope and evidence

Captured Night Order, The Back Nine, Under the Green and The Final Cut HUD, pause, settings and defeat states at 2560×1600, 1280×720 and 390×844. Also captured Night Order controls and victory. Evidence is in `output/aesthetic-audit/`; capture reports recorded no page errors. Inspected representative screenshots and corresponding CSS/HTML. This was not a full-route traversal or performance benchmark.

Result screens were staged through existing test hooks or a browser-local test hook, rather than natural deaths/completions. They establish presentation, not gameplay correctness. Night Order responsive captures were rerun with a render after each viewport change. Other chapters' original resize captures should not establish canvas-resize defects. Final Cut defeat was captured with comfort settings expanded; its height is not evidence of default defeat-screen overflow.

## Recommended sequence and performance constraints

First unify pause/settings/results, then HUD and prompt hierarchy, then targeted art/material refinements. Most UI work can use shared CSS tokens, reusable static components and state-driven updates. Avoid adding new full-screen animation loops, live blur or extra WebGL scenes. Keep native input semantics, keyboard/controller focus and reduced-motion behavior. Any later implementation should verify active and idle costs separately; this review makes no new performance claims.

Different environments, vehicles, water signals, gameplay mechanics, semantic danger colors and the intentional branching chapter order are not themselves inconsistencies. Preserve the approved intro animation and lead-in.
