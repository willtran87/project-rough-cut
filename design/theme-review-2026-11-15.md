## Seven remaining findings, in priority order

1. **Post-credits notice loses text contrast on paper.** Final Cut's “Next review: scheduled.” heading and automated-message label inherit pale text from the dark panel theme, almost disappearing on the pale paper. Give paper documents explicit dark ink tokens and check headings, labels and links inside them. The paper itself is an intentional thematic material and can stay light. Evidence: `final-cut-notice-2560.png`, `final-cut-notice-390.png`; shared heading rules in game-theme.css and notice-paper rules in final-cut.css.

2. **Recovered records lack a common visual hierarchy.** Back Nine displays collected logs as an always-expanded text block, with titles styled almost like body copy. Under the Green has a very wide, small-text quote in a disclosure; Final Cut uses a large illustrated disclosure. Use shared record headings, readable line lengths, body scale, margins and disclosure treatment while retaining each document's content. Evidence: the three `*-collected-journal-1280.png` captures. This concerns populated records, not the previously accepted empty Night Order journal.

3. **The load-limit diagram's illustration style is an outlier.** Final Cut's diagram uses smooth, thick vector strokes, a simplified cartoon vehicle and large untextured fills, unlike the menu's restrained engraved equipment drawings and detailed pixel artwork. Rework it as an authored maintenance drawing with consistent line weights, a more recognizable mower silhouette, subtle paper/ink texture and crisp lettering. Preserve explanatory clarity. Also constrain its desktop reading size: at1280×720 only the upper part fits initially, while the phone presentation shows the complete diagram. Evidence: `final-cut-collected-journal-1280.png`, `final-cut-collected-journal-390.png`; web/assets/final-cut-load-limit.svg.

4. **Night Order still has two visually different settings systems.** The primary Settings view uses uppercase category/row labels, boxed cards, percentages at the upper right and serif footer actions. Controls & comfort uses sentence-case tabs, fieldset-like sections and values below sliders; later chapters use that second family. Align row anatomy, label casing, typography, toggle/value presentation and category treatment. The preceding fix repaired orphaned headings; it did not unify these separate settings renderers. Evidence: `night-order-primary-settings-2560.png`, `night-order-camera-settings-1280.png`; course-settings.css/UI and game-theme.css/JS.

5. **Primary Settings still has excessive vertical empty space.** At2560×1600 it stretches almost the full stage height even when Audio only contains six controls. This creates a large empty area and separates footer actions from the settings they affect. Give the card a content-sensitive height with a bounded scroll region, matching other menu panels. This is a layout issue distinct from item4's control styling. Evidence: `night-order-primary-settings-2560.png`; `.course-settings-card` height100%.

6. **Listening mode retains a separate HUD treatment.** Its compass, cadence message and clue list form three broad blue-green bars, while the refined objective/resources use tighter forest/brass framing. Apply the same border, fill, spacing and caption hierarchy to the listening stack, with a sensible desktop maximum width. Preserve the existing information, scrolling and clue disclosure. Evidence: `night-order-listening-2560.png`, `night-order-listening-390.png`; `.q-listening-*` in quality-ui.css. This is an additional contextual state beyond the ordinary HUD widgets updated last time.

7. **Credits and the final notice retain gameplay-screen furniture.** Field notes, chapter links and checkpoint-save messaging remain visible alongside the closing material; Return to ending sits inside the body while Main menu is detached in the bottom footer. Compose these as dedicated closing screens with coherent actions and only relevant metadata. Back Nine's paused footer likewise still says “Time waits until you begin,” wording carried from the launch screen. Evidence: `final-cut-credits-1280.png`, `final-cut-notice-2560.png`, `back-nine-collected-journal-1280.png`.

## Scope and performance

Prioritize the paper contrast defect, then records/settings consistency. The remaining findings are refinements, not reasons for another global redesign. The menu, approved intro/reveal/lead-in, chapter-specific lighting and different vehicle/on-foot mechanics do not need changes for this list. Most fixes should be static CSS/markup; the diagram can be a single cached asset. No new particles, render passes or continuous UI animation are needed. Performance has not been rebenchmarked in this review.
