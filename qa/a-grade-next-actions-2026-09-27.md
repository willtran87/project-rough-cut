Adjust only confusing landmarks using silhouette, localized lighting or one distinctive prop; keep exit lanes clear. **A evidence:** no unresolved prop/view defect, and at least 8/10 new testers recognize visited zones. Save full-resolution before/after views and recognition responses. Maps to A-ART-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-05 — Prove gameplay readability under pressure (P0).** Owner: UI/art + accessibility/playtest QA. Reduce clutter or strengthen silhouettes/edges where identification fails; retain text/shape cues for required states. **A evidence:** 9/10 identify both object and lane within five seconds; no essential clipping or color-only meaning; critical text meets the existing approximately 16 CSS px target at the smallest supported landscape viewport. Recheck all four reference sizes and 130% UI scale. Maps to A-VIS-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-06 — Validate one clear action and every menu path (P0).** Owner: UI engineering + device/playtest QA. Have players use menu, settings, bindings, pause, defeat, victory and rehearsal on actual supported inputs. Exercise long bindings, back/reset, device switching and simultaneous lesson/threat/filing messages. Fix the specific wording, hierarchy, focus or hit-target failure observed. **A evidence:** every supported input can enter and exit every screen; no conflicting critical instructions, stale prompts or unreachable actions; 9/10 correctly state the immediate next action. Preserve the completed 42-capture/pointer regression coverage. Maps to A-UI-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-07 — Validate enemy animation and state recognition (P1).** Owner: animation/art + QA. Record Joe stopping, starting, orbiting, turning and pursuing from front, rear and both sides. Refine a pose, transition or sound only where it misleads; add intermediate views only for demonstrated snapping. **A evidence:** no obvious sliding, floating, direction chatter or mower clipping; at least 8/10 identify the three broad states without HUD help; reduced motion retains readable intent. Maps to A-ANIM-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-08 — Tune physical movement and camera comfort (P0).** Owner: controls engineering + device/playtest QA; depends on device selection in A27-02. Record device-specific results rather than allowing desktop success to stand in for touch. Maps to A-CTRL-1/2.

## Gameplay and understanding

- [ ] **A27-09 — Make the win condition self-explanatory (P0).** Owner: game design/UI + playtest QA. From first-session failures, identify whether players miss a check, misunderstand access, mistake a bonus for a requirement or fail to understand filing. Correct that step with one contextual world/HUD cue. Audit complete routes for redundant mandatory actions and unnecessary backtracking while preserving planned retreat, quiet stamp and remote diversion. **A evidence:** 8/10 explain “three checks → one access item → escape” within 60 seconds; 7/10 complete Standard within three attempts without coaching; all three stations produce a recognizable tactical decision. Maps to A-LOOP-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-10 — Close onboarding gaps at the point of confusion (P0).** Owner: game design/UI + playtest QA. Observe movement, looking, distraction and cover during the first three minutes. Change the failing cue or practice layout, then verify skip, first miss, replay, device switch and return visits. Keep mastery retirement tied to demonstrated actions and immediate interactions above basic lessons. **A evidence:** 8/10 demonstrate all four skills within three minutes uncoached; skipping never blocks check one; returning players retain mastery and can deliberately replay help. Maps to A-LEARN-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-11 — Make off-route navigation recoverable (P0).** Owner: level design + QA. Review player detours and approach every mandatory station/access item/exit from both sides during calm and pursuit. Fix misleading gaps, geometry-pointing cues or stale destinations. Then ask familiar players to navigate with Route Thread hidden and recover through Listen/map. **A evidence:** existing 63 approach checks remain passing; no observed trapping/lost episode exceeds 30 seconds without an understandable recovery cue; 8/10 returning testers navigate a familiar section using landmarks. Maps to A-NAV-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-12 — Make every reviewed capture explainable (P0).** Owner: AI/gameplay engineering + playtest QA. Match sight, proximity, sound, tracks, wake and station-signal events to visible pose, audio, warning and defeat explanation. Review capture clips for a feasible response, distinguishing deliberate contact from fresh distant acquisition. Correct the specific warning, geometry or encounter when counterplay is absent; verify rehearsal recreates the useful lesson. **A evidence:** cause/timing assertions match feedback; no reviewed unavoidable capture from an unannounced transition; at least 8/10 explain their capture and a plausible counteraction. Preserve the fresh-acquisition and solid-cover tests. Maps to A-AI-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-13 — Shape tension and recovery across every variant (P1).** Owner: game design + playtest QA. Annotate full-run recordings alongside phase logs for danger stacking, empty travel and idle stretches over ten seconds. Distinguish purposeful scouting from confusion. Adjust route connections, patrol timing, signals or earned relief only at identified weak stretches; verify optional events do not repeatedly interrupt required actions. **A evidence:** observable opening, escalation and finish with usable recovery; no unexplained forced idle >10 seconds without a useful decision; 8/10 rate pacing ≥4/5. Maps to A-PACE-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-14 — Make rewards understood and worth choosing (P1).** Owner: game design/UI/audio + playtest QA. Log benefits misunderstood, rewards ignored and optional risks treated as mandatory. Clarify cost before commitment and exact payoff afterward; tune benefit, travel or danger only when observations justify it. Review check/mastery/escape feedback for a short, distinct payoff without locking controls. **A evidence:** 8/10 explain an earned benefit and an optional risk they declined; advertised benefits apply exactly once; inventory/cancellation/retry checks pass; no-bonus completions remain satisfying in the study. Maps to A-REWARD-1/2.

- [ ] **A27-15 — Make another run offer a different plan (P2).** Owner: game design + returning-player QA. Compare players' actual plans across variants and exits. Where they repeat the same plan without a meaningful choice, adjust the briefing or measured route/patrol weakness. Verify results offer one understandable mastery challenge, quick retry and full-run return after rehearsal. **A evidence:** returning players describe a materially different plan for another variant; at least 7/10 voluntarily retry when stopping is equally easy and name a tactical/mastery reason; equivalent-rule records stay isolated. Maps to A-REPLAY-1/2. No forced streak or loss penalty is needed.

## Evidence and completion rules

For each ticket record: status, responsible role/person, source and asset revision, actual device or anonymous participant IDs, reproduction, changed files, before/after evidence, failed runs, acceptance results and remaining limitations. Suggested statuses: open → evidence collected → fix implemented → verified; mark a checkbox only at verified.

The same study can supply several area measurements without creating separate cohorts for each row. Assess immediate-object recognition, win-condition explanation, skills, captures, rewards and replay separately. Preserve the baseline before teaching, HUD-hidden clip tests or design changes contaminate it. Physical testing and real people cannot be replaced by synthetic inputs or favorable screenshots.
