Search expires within ten seconds of the last evidence; he then follows the course patrol rather than a hidden player. Probe order alternates between searches, but retries start consistently. No new per-frame navigation probes or visibility queries.
- Fresh sight or sound interrupts search. A bell records its actual position rather than inheriting the player's position. Search yields to committed attacks, post-ram retreat, checkpoint/ejection protection, alarm diversion and announced detours.
- Ram windup now uses remembered heading/speed/gap. Hidden turns no longer steer or pace the windup. A completed retreat with stale evidence returns to investigation instead of aiming another blind charge. Existing visible warnings, long locked foot charges, contacts, steering and escape windows remain.
- Quiet search suppresses optional tension stings, pressure waves, omens and dialogue. Leaving sight range does not restart them. The existing relief arrangement fades the driving pulse, while the real engine and residual world sound remain audible. Fresh evidence restores pursuit and its sound. Optional sound captions describe the slowing/idling mower; ordinary banners remain quiet.
