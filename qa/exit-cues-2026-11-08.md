# Shed and drain exit visibility

Both entrances now have fixed weathered pixel boards with a lock or exit-door symbol, an explicit status, and a short requirement/action line. The shed board replaces its old nameboard; the drain board spans its stone shoulders and replaces the overlapping freestanding label. Segmented doorway/arch reflectors and paired threshold marks brighten modestly when that particular route is ready. The surrounding lighting and fog remain unchanged.

States are derived independently for each exit from the three mandatory checks plus the shed key or drain valve. Boards distinguish LOCKED (checks and access missing), NEED KEY/VALVE, CHECKS, READY, FILING and OPENING. Cancellation returns to READY without losing prerequisites. The text never promises safety from Joe. Existing nearby prompts retain the player's current Use binding and filing progress. The old single-route pulsing beacon is suppressed in the 3D scene to avoid conflicting cues.
