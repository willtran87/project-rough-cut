# Haunted course: pacing, personalities, atmosphere and escape

## Implemented

- Ambient sightings begin with a 1.6-second hush before appearing, last 13–15 seconds including that warning, then leave 14–20 seconds of recovery. Existing objective accents and protected player actions suppress sightings immediately. The scheduler retains one apparition, stable spawn anchors and pause/reset behavior. Recovery restores natural ambience; the real mower remains on its separate authoritative audio path.
- Watchers hold under a direct gaze and creep at most 1.6 world meters along their checked lane after looking away for .45 seconds. Followers respond to actual player translation, then settle over a fraction of a step when the player stops. Deceivers retain four rush rhythms and can briefly reverse their withdrawal. Existing obstacle/mower clearance, committed targets, live-player clearance and finite lifetimes remain. Lost and Found explains the silhouettes and counterplay.
- Eight course zones use distinct restrained fog/moon palettes and mist height/spread. Blending spans 24 course units around boundaries and follows position, so returning through a boundary or enabling Reduced Motion does not jump between filters. Existing art, lights, shaders and capped mist batches are reused. Floodlight color/intensity synchronization is unchanged.
- Prepared exits have a steady gold beacon and short approach marks, including during Reduced Motion and filing. Final approach quiets incidental ambience. After at least eight seconds, one optional release-desk clack can attract Joe back to the completed desk via normal investigation/pathfinding. It only starts while distant, settled and free of other commitments; it expires after 35 seconds if unsafe. Chase, hearing/sight, player lures, map and filing retain priority. No mandatory chase, exit lock, teleport, new objective or changed filing duration.
