# Architecture and woodland atmosphere

This pass refines the existing 3D buildings using the original shed's weathered timber, corrugated metal, mossy footings and maintenance clutter as reference. It also replaces the empty ground beneath the border trees with a continuous woodland setting. It does not introduce new enterable buildings or change gameplay collision, movement, navigation, interaction or Joe.

## Changes

- Built-in ImageGen produced one 1536x1024 surface atlas: timber, rusted metal, fieldstone, plaster, slate and forest floor. Source and exact prompt: `assets/environment/architecture/`. Runtime: `web/assets/optimized/rough-cut-architecture-surfaces-v1.webp`, lossless WebP, 2.61 MiB. No CLI fallback. Materials use nearest magnification, mipmaps and the existing discrete light shading. These are surfaces applied to real models, not pictures substituted for buildings.
- Roof sheets now have curved corrugations, slightly irregular edges, fasteners and a curved ridge cap. Slate courses have staggered joints and small height variations. Timber boards have subtle bowing, unequal weathered ends and different grain offsets. Narrow architectural components keep inexpensive sharp-edge topology.
- The sheds gain exposed rafter ends, a recessed side window, gable louvres, a leaning rake, soft stacked sacks with curved seams and moss around the masonry footings. The existing door, filing box and warm tool-room interior remain functional. Clubhouse lower walls and chimneys use the fieldstone surface; plaster and slate carry finer material detail.
- `web/pixel-landscape.js` creates rolling off-course ground, irregular vegetation clusters, rounded mossy stones, broken edging, fallen timber and layered trees. Clearings retain views of the clubhouse and service yard. The terrain wraps behind the exit; shared height calculations ground trees and plants and avoid a side/end seam. The foreground edge varies slightly rather than forming a straight rectangular texture strip.
- Vegetation consists of fixed crossed plant components around actual terrain and stones. Existing distant parallax layers remain intact. Static parts use the current spatial/material batching and rendering-preparation gate. Runtime asset validation now scans JavaScript modules so the new atlas is included in the shipped-image inventory.
