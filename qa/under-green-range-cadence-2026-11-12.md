# Under the Green: continuous range timing

The range's route choices must be compared through the keeper room, not only to the middle landing. The original B offset of 1 and keeper offset of 4 made the wet branch lose its local advantage downstream. A continuous walking baseline across twelve arrival offsets, with live Joe and no per-channel resets, showed no meaningful wet advantage at the exit. The final comparison baseline uses crossing advice for the mandatory keeper channel on both branches and has no water hits; its best wet advantage is a single simulation frame, while unfavorable wet arrivals lose nearly twelve seconds.

## Runtime change

Only two authored phase offsets change in `web/under-green-layout.js`: B from 1 to 4, and keeper from 4 to 3.25. A retains offset 7. Within the existing twelve-second period, B opens three seconds after A; the keeper opens 0.75 seconds after B. Each still has its existing low, warning, surge and recession durations. Signals, audio, guidance, player water contact and Joe's route eligibility consume the same state.

The keeper adjustment matters for cautious movement. Changing B alone helped walking and sprinting but left the cautious wet route without a useful time advantage. The staged final window lets a timely crossing preserve its gain without making all three outlets match. Movement, enemy speeds, attack reach, safe-window duration, collision geometry and progress persistence are unchanged.

## Comparison method and retained failures

`tools/probe-under-green-folded.mjs` now supports ordinary crouched input and an explicit `timedKeeper` policy. This policy adds near/far keeper crossing goals and uses the same crossing-advice calculation as the range outlets. It is enabled for both branches in the new balance tests. The legacy path pilot remains available: it reasons about present water occupancy and can enter a channel too late. Its results remain useful for recovery, but those avoidable hits should not bias the balance comparison.

Retained reports in `output/under-green/range-cadence/` include the original four B candidates, the B-only candidate's failed cautious comparison, two keeper probes, and the legacy pilot's final-schedule comparison. The failures were investigated rather than removed. For example, the legacy dry crouch pilot at offset 9.5 was caught after a late approach and repeated evasions; the advice-following pilot completes that arrival in 42.10 seconds with no water hit or attack evasion. This proves a usable input route, not that the legacy pilot's defeat was unavoidable.

Authoritative new route comparisons live under `output/under-green/range-cadence/advised/`. The baseline is reproducible with `node tools/probe-under-green-range-cadence.mjs --advised`; it temporarily uses the old schedule only inside the test process. `verify-under-green-range-balance.mjs` compares 24 half-second pump-checkpoint arrivals at walking, sprinting and crouching speeds, on both branches. It records complete travel, waiting by outlet, actual crossing entries/clears, water hits, attacks, proximity and outcomes. `verify-under-green-range-arrivals.mjs` adds fresh starts across all three pressure profiles, cautious middle-landing retries and selected pump retries on the other pressure profiles.

## Limits and next work

These pilots know the map, phases and attack state. A caught fixed-policy pilot does not establish an unavoidable death, and an automated completion does not establish enjoyment or first-time comprehension. No enemy is repositioned or reset between channels. Retain separate coverage for mixed branch choices, reverse/lateral entries, occupied recovery banks and broader pressure-profile arrival envelopes. Actual-device, unfamiliar-player and listening acceptance remain open.
